Focus

Research Interests

My research centers on large multimodal foundation models — vision-language, video-language, and increasingly agentic models that plan and act in digital and physical environments — and aims to make them sustainable to manage and reliable to deploy without costly retraining. I pursue this goal along three complementary directions: unlearning and continual learning, faithful and efficient inference, and agentic and embodied AI.

Unlearning and Continual Learning

I develop methods for changing what a large model already knows — removing facts it should forget, absorbing skills it lacks — with a growing focus on structures that keep knowledge localized and composable rather than entangled.

Faithful and Efficient Inference

I work on keeping multimodal outputs grounded in the intended evidence — addressing hallucination and cross-modal imbalance — while designing inference-time compression that scales with input complexity instead of breaking under it.

Agentic and Embodied AI

As foundation models move from passive perception to autonomous action, I study how they can plan, use tools, and act reliably — as digital agents navigating multi-step tasks or embodied vision-language-action models in the physical world.
